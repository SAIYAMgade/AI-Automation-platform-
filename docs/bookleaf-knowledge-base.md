## Getting Started

### Challenge Overview

**How do I join the 21-Day Writing Challenge?**
Authors can join from the BookLeaf challenge page, complete registration, and pay the publishing package fee.

**What is included in the Rs. 1999 publishing package?**
The Rs. 1999 package covers challenge participation and core publishing workflow access such as dashboard access, guided poem submission, cover setup tools, basic book setup, and publishing workflow support. Premium add-ons are separate unless explicitly included.

**Is this challenge genuine?**
The challenge is intended to help authors complete a poetry manuscript with daily writing momentum and move into BookLeaf's publishing workflow.

**Can I use a pen name?**
Yes. The pen name should be used consistently across final author metadata, cover, dashboard submission, and publishing records.

**What if I want to publish without joining the challenge?**
Authors who do not want the challenge can still publish through BookLeaf's assisted publishing workflow when that service path is available.

## Payments & Refunds

**What does the Rs. 1999 cover?**
It covers the core challenge and publishing workflow access. It does not automatically include advanced PR, award submissions, bestseller campaigns, extra copies, or custom services.

**Can I get a refund if I change my mind?**
Refund eligibility depends on the active payment policy and whether publishing work has already started. Support must verify the payment stage before promising a refund.

**Can I upgrade later with add-on services?**
Authors can usually upgrade later with add-ons such as editing, cover design, PR, award support, bestseller package, and additional copies if the workflow stage allows it.

## Dashboard & Submission

### Login & Access

**I did not receive login details.**
Check spam and promotions folders first. If still missing, contact support with the registered email or phone number.

**I forgot my password.**
Use the password reset option on the dashboard login page. If the reset email is not received, support should verify identity and trigger a manual reset.

### Writing & Saving Poems

**How do I submit poems?**
Submit poems in the dashboard submission area and save progress after each poem.

**Can I write in Hindi?**
Hindi poems are acceptable when the dashboard and publishing workflow support the selected script/font.

**How do I save progress?**
Use the dashboard save option after each poem or major edit.

**Can I rearrange poems later?**
Rearranging is usually possible before final submission. After final submission, changes may need support review.

### Finalizing Submission

**What happens after I submit all poems?**
BookLeaf starts editorial QA, cover checks, metadata validation, ISBN/listing steps, and production scheduling.

**How do I review and finish my submission?**
Review poem order, spelling, author details, cover information, and back cover text before finalizing.

## Cover Design & Book Setup

### Cover Creator Tool

**How do I design my cover?**
Choose an available template, update title and author name, and review the preview.

**Can I upload my own cover design?**
Only if the selected package/workflow supports custom cover upload and the file meets print requirements.

**Why do I see only 4 templates?**
Those are the templates currently available for that workflow.

### Back Cover & Author Info

**What should I write on the back cover?**
Briefly describe the book, theme, or author voice.

**Can I upload my profile photo?**
Yes, when the dashboard provides that option and the image meets quality guidelines.

**How do I edit or remove back cover text?**
Edit before final submission. After finalization, support should verify whether production has started.

## Publishing & Distribution

### Publishing Timeline

**How long does it take to publish my book?**
The timeline depends on final submission date, proof review, cover readiness, metadata validation, ISBN/listing steps, and add-ons.

**What is the timeline with the Rs. 8899 add-on?**
The Rs. 8899 add-on may provide a faster or enhanced workflow when available, but the exact timeline should be checked against the author's package and production queue.

### Sales & Royalties

**Where will my book be available?**
Books may be available through BookLeaf-supported sales and distribution channels after publishing and listing approval.

**What is the royalty rate?**
Royalty rate depends on the author's package and sales channel terms.

**When will I get my royalty report?**
Royalty reports are shared after sales data is reconciled. Account-specific status must be checked in author records.

## Policies & Support

### Ownership & Copyright

**Who owns the rights to my book?**
Authors retain ownership of their original writing unless a separate written agreement says otherwise. Legal or copyright disputes should be escalated.

### Technical Issues

**My dashboard is not working.**
Refresh, clear cache, switch browser/device, and check internet connectivity. If unresolved, contact support with registered email, screenshot, and issue description.

**Can I submit via email if the portal is not loading?**
Email submission should be treated as an exception and accepted only after support confirms the portal issue and author identity.
